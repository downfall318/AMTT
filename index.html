Reveal is a separate note-first introduction and review phase. Introduction, Utilization, and Weaknesses use the fixed exercise order. Reveal introduces each note, interval, and chord three times in a row, then reviews every item once per round for eight shuffled rounds. The question total grows before Reveal ends.
