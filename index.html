Choose an entry point

Reveal is a separate note-first introduction and review phase. Introduction, Utilization, and Weaknesses use the fixed exercise order. Reveal introduces each note, interval, and chord three times in a row, then reviews each item in eight shuffled rounds. Utilization mixes familiar and newly introduced material across the full note pool. The question total grows before Reveal ends. Guided Introduction shortens sections when only a few distinct targets are available. Completion indicators last only for this session.
